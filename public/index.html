❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Vortex Client.

What is Vortex? ▼
Vortex is a Minecraft Bedrock client that connects through Vortex Proxy. It gives you access to 38+ modules across combat, movement, visual, utility, and HUD categories. You authenticate with your real Microsoft account — Vortex never sees your password.
What platforms does Vortex support? ▼
Vortex supports Minecraft Bedrock Edition. The proxy connection works with any Bedrock client — you point your Minecraft to the Vortex Proxy address after connecting through the website.
How do I connect Microsoft? ▼
Click Connect Microsoft anywhere on the site. You will be redirected to Microsoft's official login page at login.microsoftonline.com — Vortex never sees your password. After logging in, you are redirected back and your account is linked.
How does TPMine work? ▼
TPMine targets ore blocks only. Use /.tpmine diamond_ore to set a target ore. Then crouch/sneak in Minecraft — Vortex will teleport you to the nearest matching ore in range. TPMine only works with valid ores: diamond, emerald, gold, iron, coal, copper, redstone, lapis, ancient debris, and other Minecraft ores. If you enter a non-ore block like dirt or stone, Vortex will respond: TPMine only supports ores.
What is included with Premium? ▼
Vortex Premium includes six exclusive modules: InfiniteAura, AntiHits, StashFinder, AutoMine, AutoArmor, and AutoTotem. These are the only Premium modules — all other modules are available to regular users.
How do I connect to a server? ▼
After connecting your Microsoft account, go to your Account page. Choose a server from the available list, then click Connect Vortex Proxy. You will get a Vortex Proxy address — add that as an external server in Minecraft Bedrock and you are in.
How do I purchase Premium? ▼
Click Get Vortex Premium on the Premium section. You will be taken to Stripe checkout — the link will be live when Vortex launches. Your Premium status is automatically applied to your account after payment.
